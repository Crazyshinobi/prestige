Prestige Group

Prestige Group is one of the leading real estate developers in India, with a strong presence in the southern part of the country, particularly in cities like Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi, and Mysore. Founded in 1986 by Razack Sattar, the group has established itself as a trusted name in the real estate industry, known for its commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Over the years, the Prestige Group has diversified its portfolio to include residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, and leisure developments. Some of their key projects include residential apartments, villas, luxury homes, IT parks, SEZs (Special Economic Zones), shopping malls, hotels, and resorts.

The group has earned numerous awards and accolades for its projects, including the CNBC Awaaz Real Estate Award, Asia Pacific Property Award, and the FIABCI Prix d'Excellence Award, among others.

Prestige Group is renowned for its focus on sustainability and green initiatives in its developments. Many of its projects are designed to meet international standards for energy efficiency and environmental sustainability.

Overall, the Prestige Group is recognized for its commitment to excellence, integrity, and delivering world-class real estate projects that cater to the evolving needs of its customers.

Siddharth Vihar, NH24

Located in the thriving locality of Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, Prestige Siddharth Vihar stands as a beacon of luxury and elegance. Spanning an expansive 62.5 acres, this prestigious project offers a serene yet connected lifestyle, perfectly blending modern architecture with natural beauty. With a range of meticulously designed 2, 3, 4, and 5 BHK residences, Prestige Siddharth Vihar is crafted to meet the needs of discerning homebuyers. The spacious apartments, starting from 1240* sq. ft. for 2 BHKs, going up to a grand 5000* sq. ft. for 5 BHKs, ensure ample living space and luxurious comfort for you and your family.

Prestige Siddharth Vihar is strategically situated along NH 24, providing excellent connectivity to Delhi, Noida, and other parts of NCR. The premium location of Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, offers a perfect blend of convenience and tranquility, with close proximity to top educational institutions, healthcare facilities, shopping centers, and entertainment hubs. The project boasts world-class amenities, including three exclusive clubhouses, each offering a unique set of recreational and wellness facilities. With a well-planned layout of 45 floors, Prestige Siddharth Vihar offers stunning views of the cityscape, making it an ideal home for those seeking an elevated lifestyle.

The project’s flexible CLP (Construction Linked Payment) plan ensures that your investment is secure and tailored to your financial needs. At Prestige Siddharth Vihar, every detail has been thoughtfully curated to offer a living experience like no other. Whether you are looking for a spacious 2 BHK or a grand 5 BHK, this project promises to deliver on every front, ensuring that you enjoy the best of both worlds—luxury living and a premium location—starting at just ₹1.48 Cr*.
